App EQMS - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

App EQMS is a personal testing project for a WhatsApp-based queue management chatbot.

Information We Process

During testing, the chatbot may process WhatsApp phone numbers, messages, selected branches, selected services, and queue ticket details.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to chatbot requests, test queue ticket issuance, and provide ticket status updates.

Data Sharing

Information may be processed through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and the connected queue management system used for testing. We do not sell personal information.

Data Storage and Security

Test records may be stored in the chatbot's database and application logs. Access is restricted to the application operator. Reasonable measures are used to protect the test environment.

Data Retention and Deletion

Test information is retained only as needed for testing and troubleshooting. Individuals may request access to or deletion of their information using the contact method below, subject to applicable requirements.
